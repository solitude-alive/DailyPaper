These results, coupled with the fact that PatchCat works with small, local LLMs, are a promising step toward interpretable, efficient, and green GI. We outline a rich agenda of future work and call for the community to join our vision of building a principled understanding of LLM-driven mutations, guiding the GI search process with semantic signals.

- **Abstract**: 3D inpainting often relies on multi-view 2D image inpainting, where the inherent inconsistencies across different inpainted views can result in blurred textures, spatial discontinuities, and distracting visual artifacts. These inconsistencies pose significant challenges when striving for accurate and realistic 3D object completion, particularly in applications that demand high fidelity and structural coherence. To overcome these limitations, we propose ObjFiller-3D, a novel method designed for the completion and editing of high-quality and consistent 3D objects. - **Summary**: Here's a summary and critical evaluation of the paper "ObjFiller-3D: Consistent Multi-view 3D Inpainting via Video Diffusion Models":

**Summary:**

The paper introduces ObjFiller-3D, a novel approach for 3D object inpainting using video diffusion models.  Instead of directly inpainting 2D views independently and then reconstructing the 3D object (which often leads to inconsistencies), the method adapts a pre-trained video diffusion model to enforce inter-frame consistency across multiple views during the inpainting process. This is achieved by treating the rendered views of the 3D object as frames in a video and then leveraging the temporal coherence capabilities of video diffusion models. The authors also introduce a reference-based inpainting method, allowing users to guide the process using a complete reference image, particularly useful in scenarios like restoring historical artifacts.  The paper demonstrates that ObjFiller-3D outperforms existing 3D inpainting methods in terms of reconstruction quality, consistency, and runtime, claiming state-of-the-art results across benchmark datasets.  The approach also extends to 3D scene inpainting and object editing.

Existing approaches primarily rely on 2D image inpainting followed by 3D reconstruction, or 3D-specific diffusion models with limited view consistency. Leveraging the inherent temporal coherence of video models to improve multi-view consistency for 3D inpainting is a meaningful contribution.
